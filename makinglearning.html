Making + Learning

I like learning new things, making things with other people, and creating spaces where people can learn together. Here are a few of the things I've been learning recently!

CS for All Washington Podcast

I hosted the CS for All Washington podcast , talking with Washington educators about joyful, equitable, and identity-inclusive computer science education.

CS Education Book Club

I organized and facilitated a book club for computer science educators interested in reading and talking together about computing, education, equity, and justice. You can see the books we read together on StoryGraph .

Learning lately
