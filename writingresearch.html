Writing + Research

I'm interested in how computing classrooms can make more space for students' identities, languages, cultures, creativity, and knowledge. The questions I care about come directly from my teaching, and my recent research has grown out of an ongoing collaboration with the University of Washington's Center for Learning, Computing, and Imagination.

Adolescent Identity Expression through Transdisciplinary, Computational, Creative Writing

In a six-week course with high school students, we combined creative writing and creative coding using Wordplay, a programming language for creating multilingual interactive typography. We found that students used code to express aspects of their identities that text alone couldn't capture, while the integration also created new pathways between writing and computing.
